Unearth privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Unearth is a daily word puzzle that runs inside Reddit posts, built on Reddit’s Developer Platform (Devvit). It is an independent app, not made or endorsed by Reddit. This page says exactly what the app keeps about you, what it sends elsewhere, and for how long. See also the terms of use.

What we store

Everything is kept in the app’s own Redis database on Reddit’s platform, separately for each subreddit the app is installed in. You are identified by your Reddit user ID (the t2_ number), never by your username.

What Linked to you? Why
Your run for the day: the answers you typed, which were accepted or rejected, your score and pickaxes left Yes, by user ID So you can resume a run and see your end screen
Your streak (days in a row and the last day you played), your deepest mineral, and whether you have dismissed the first-run hints or seen the join prompt Yes, by user ID Streaks, the optional flair, and not repeating hints
Whether you posted your result as a comment today Yes, by user ID So the comment button is only used once per run
Counts of each accepted answer per layer, and how many players reached or stopped at each layer No, totals only Rarity scores and the day’s statistics
Cached answer checks: an answer’s text and whether it fitted the clues No So the same answer is not checked twice

Your sound on/off setting is kept in your own browser’s local storage, not on a server.

Other players only ever see answers from the puzzle’s hand-written answer list, never anything else you type. Your username is read from Reddit only to show it to you before you post a comment or set flair; it is not stored by the app.

What we send to Google Gemini

Most answers are checked against the day’s hand-written answer list and never leave Reddit. When you type an answer that is not on the list and has not been checked before, the app sends it to the Google Gemini API to ask whether it fits the clues. The request contains only the day’s clues up to your current layer and your answer (lowercased, punctuation removed, at most 40 characters). It contains no username, user ID or other information about you.

We use Gemini’s paid service, through a Google Cloud project with billing enabled. Under Google’s Gemini API Additional Terms of Service, Google does not use prompts or responses from paid services to improve its products, and keeps them for a limited time only to detect abuse. Google is the only outside service that receives anything from Unearth.

What happens on Reddit when you press a button

Three buttons on the end screen act as you on Reddit, and only when you press them: Discuss in comments posts your spoiler-free result as a comment under your username; Join subscribes you to the subreddit; the flair button sets your flair in that subreddit to your streak and deepest mineral. Comments are ordinary Reddit comments: you can edit or delete them on Reddit at any time.

The app also sends Reddit progress events (a run started, a layer cleared, a run finished, and its score) through Reddit’s own analytics for apps. They contain no answers or usernames.

How long we keep it

Every record above is set to delete itself 30 days after it was last changed. If you stop playing, or delete your Reddit account, all data linked to your user ID is gone within 30 days.

What we never do

  • Sell, share or license your data.
  • Use it, or let anyone use it, to train AI models.
  • Ask for passwords, real names, email addresses or locations.
  • Track you outside the Unearth post.

Your choices and contact

You can stop playing at any time; the app never posts, joins or sets flair without your press. To ask what is stored about you, to have it deleted sooner, or to report a problem, send a message to the moderators of r/UnearthDaily. Reddit’s own handling of your data is covered by Reddit’s privacy policy.

Reddit accounts are for people aged 13 and over, and Unearth is not aimed at anyone younger. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
