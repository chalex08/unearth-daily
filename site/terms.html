Last updated 22 November 2026

Unearth is a free daily word puzzle that runs inside Reddit posts, built on Reddit’s Developer Platform. It is an independent app, not made or endorsed by Reddit. By playing it you agree to these terms and to Reddit’s own User Agreement and rules, which apply as on the rest of Reddit. How the app handles data is in the privacy policy.

Playing

  • One puzzle a day, the same for everyone, one run per Reddit account per day. Using several accounts to replay or to change the day’s statistics is not allowed.
  • Type answers that fit the clues. Do not type slurs, personal information or anything that breaks Reddit’s rules. Rejected answers are never shown to other players.
  • Some answers are checked by an AI service (Google Gemini). It can be wrong. Scores, rarity and streaks are for fun and have no value of any kind.
  • Do not try to break, overload or reverse-engineer the app, or to read puzzles before their day.

Posting as you

The end screen can post your result as a comment under your username, join the subreddit for you, or set your flair. Each happens only when you press its own button. What you post is your content and follows Reddit’s rules; you can delete it on Reddit at any time.

The puzzles

The clues, answer lists, artwork and sounds are made for Unearth. You may share your results and talk about the puzzle freely; please keep today’s answers out of titles and spoiler-free share text.
