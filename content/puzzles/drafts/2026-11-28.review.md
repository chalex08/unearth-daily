- Clue 10 "whose country's name is also a lake" works for Malawi (Lake Malawi). Chad (Lake Chad) also fits the wording, but N'Djamena is out by layer 6 (not English-official). A player might wonder which way round the naming goes.
- Clue 3 "in the tropics" is judged by the capital's latitude (within 23.4° of the Equator). Havana (23.1°N) is in; Dhaka (23.8°N), Muscat, Gaborone (24.7°S) and Maputo are out. A player could read it as "hot country".
- Clue 5 "inland, not on the coast" is judged on the capital itself, not the country. Borderline: Kinshasa and Brazzaville (on the Congo, counted inland), Asmara (counted inland), Porto-Novo and Nouakchott (counted coastal).
- Clue 2 "republic": Samoa (Apia) counted as a republic; Vatican City, Andorra, Torshavn and Nuuk counted as not. Jerusalem, Taipei and Pristina are in layer 1 and count as republics.
- Clue 6 (English official): Tanzania (Dodoma), Rwanda, Burundi, Cameroon and Seychelles counted as yes; Khartoum and Port Louis as no, as their status is unclear.
- Burundi has two capitals, Gitega and Bujumbura; Eswatini has Mbabane and Lobamba. All are in the pool.
- Layer 1 includes disputed or non-UN entries (Jerusalem, Taipei, Pristina, Nuuk, Torshavn, Edinburgh, Cardiff, Belfast); drop any you don't want.
- "victoria" (Seychelles) is ambiguous as a bare name.
- Commonness: Lusaka and Harare uncommon, Lilongwe rare; about 21% common, 44% uncommon, 34% rare at layer 1.
