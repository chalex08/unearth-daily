# 2026-11-26 (#19, harmonica): judgement calls

- Clue 2 "a musical instrument": music box, wind chimes, whistle and voice are put at depth 1 (not instruments). Players may argue the music box and wind chimes.
- Clue 3 "hold while playing": the critic called this subjective. Cello, double bass, harp, drums, sitar, bongos and cymbals are at depth 2. Tuba, sousaphone, bassoon, accordion and didgeridoo count as held.
- Clue 4 "blow into": kazoo is at depth 3, because it's hummed rather than blown. You also draw air through a harmonica, which the critic said a pedant could object to.
- Clue 5 changed from "made of metal" to "metal on the outside", because harmonica combs are often plastic or wood. Clarinet, oboe, recorder and melodica fail it. Saxophone and flute pass.
- Clue 6 "no valves": some harmonicas have small leather "valves" (windsavers). That's trivia, and the standard diatonic passes.
- Clue 7 was changed to "no keys to press", so the musical key a harmonica is sold in ("key of C") doesn't confuse players.
- Chromatic harmonica is a separate answer at depth 7 (its slide fails clue 8), not an alias. A player who types it at layer 8 loses a pickaxe.
- Clue 10 "several notes at once" removes bugle. Brass multiphonics and multi-tone train whistles are trivia exceptions the critic mentioned; whistle is at depth 1.
