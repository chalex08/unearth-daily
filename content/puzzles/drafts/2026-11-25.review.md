# 2026-11-25 (#18, fistball): judgement calls

- How niche: the critic rated fistball "almost nobody" outside Germany, Austria, Switzerland and South America. That's the nichest final in this batch. Expect very few layer-10 solves and many players stuck at volleyball from layer 7.
- Clue 7 "played over a net": fistball officially uses a tape or rope, so a pedant could object. "Net" is kept because it's how most players picture the sport.
- Clue 9 "one bounce is allowed": jokgu (one bounce allowed) and prellball (the ball must bounce) both pass and are the layer-9 decoys.
- Clue 10 "played on grass": fistball's standard outdoor game is on grass; jokgu (clay or hard courts) and prellball (indoor gym) fail. Jokgu can be played casually on grass, which is the weakest point.
- Clue 3 "four or more players per side" means players on the field. Polo (4) passes and fails clue 4 (mallet). Beach volleyball, sepak takraw and footvolley fail at clue 3.
- Clue 6 "without a goalkeeper": netball is at depth 5 because of its "Goal Keeper" position.
- Snooker is now depth 2 (it uses balls). Badminton and ice hockey are depth 1 (shuttlecock, puck). "Hockey" is an alias of field hockey and "football" of soccer.
