# 2026-11-23 (#16, spaghetti): judgement calls

- Clue 2 "served hot": the critic called it subjective, because pizza, pie and pasta also get eaten cold. Bread, croissant, bagel, pretzel, focaccia and rolls are put at depth 1. Toast, tortilla and pita are at depth 3 (they're usually served warm).
- Clue 3 "mostly made of flour": pizza, calzone, pie, burrito and dumplings are put at depth 2 because of their fillings and toppings. Gnocchi is at depth 2 because it's mostly potato. Couscous is at depth 2. Dried pasta counts as flour (durum semolina), which the critic said some players may dispute.
- Clue 4 "boiled in water": lasagna and cannelloni are at depth 3 (baked dishes, even though the sheets are boiled first).
- Clue 5 "comes from Italy": mac and cheese is treated as American (depth 4). Spaghetti and meatballs is Italian-American. That's why clue 10 says "often served with" and doesn't claim the dish is Italian.
- Clue 8 "whose strands are round": linguine is treated as flat (depth 7). Spaghetti alla chitarra (square strands) isn't in the pool.
- Clue 9 "isn't hollow inside" only removes bucatini, a rare answer. The critic called it valid but weak.
- Clue 10 "often served with meatballs" removes vermicelli and angel hair. "Often" is a bit subjective, but the critic found nothing else that passes.
- The alias "chips" goes to fries (UK meaning, served hot); "crisps" goes to potato chips (depth 1). So a US player who types "chips" at layer 2 is accepted.
