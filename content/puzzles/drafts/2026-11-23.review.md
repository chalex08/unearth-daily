# 2026-11-23 (#16, corzetti): judgement calls

- How niche: the critic rated corzetti "enthusiasts only" (Italian-food fans). Expect many players to guess orecchiette at layers 9–10.
- Clue 7 changed from "shorter than a finger" to "a short pasta shape", because corzetti (5–6 cm across) sat right on the finger-length line. Long strands (spaghetti, linguine and the rest) fail it.
- Clue 8 "no hole through it": shells (conchiglie) and twists (fusilli, gemelli) pass. Campanelle, lumache, strozzapreti and stelline are put at depth 7, because their openings are arguable.
- Clue 9 "shaped like a coin" cuts from 20 to 2 (orecchiette, corzetti). Cencioni and dischi volanti are at depth 8 (oval or double-disc). The Polcevera corzetti (figure-eight shape) aren't in the pool.
- Clue 10 "stamped with a pattern" needs knowledge of corzetti to apply. That's intentional at the final layer, but players without it have no path to the answer.
- Earlier calls still apply: pizza, dumplings, gnocchi and couscous are at depth 2 (not mostly flour), lasagna and cannelloni are at depth 3 (baked), "chips" goes to fries, and mac and cheese counts as American.
