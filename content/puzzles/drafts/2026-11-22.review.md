# 2026-11-22 (#15, kakapo): judgement calls

- How niche: the critic rated kakapo "enthusiasts only". Most New Zealanders know it, and it has some internet fame (the "party parrot"); outside New Zealand most adults couldn't name it. Expect many players to stop at kiwi at layer 9.
- Clue 3 cuts hard, from 86 to 26 (it removes every bird that flies). Chicken and turkey are treated as able to fly (depth 3), though both only manage short hops.
- Clue 6 "lives in the Southern Hemisphere": ostrich and flightless cormorant are put at depth 5 because their ranges cross the equator.
- Clue 7 "smaller than an adult human": rhea, emu and cassowary are at depth 6. Emperor penguin (about 1.2 m) passes.
- Clue 8 "found only in New Zealand": the generic "penguin" and the little penguin fail (the little penguin also lives in Australia). Yellow-eyed, Fiordland, Snares and erect-crested penguins pass (depth 8).
- Clue 9 "active at night": weka and Auckland teal, which are partly active at dusk and dawn, are put at depth 8. Kiwi is the main decoy at layer 9.
- Clue 2 is nearly redundant with clue 3, as the critic noted, but it still removes 70 non-egg-layers at layer 2.
- Shark is at depth 1, because many sharks give live birth.
