# 2026-11-24 (#17, dandy horse): judgement calls

- How niche: the critic rated it "enthusiasts only, close to almost nobody". Many adults know there was a pedal-less early bike but can't name it. Expect wrong guesses of penny-farthing, boneshaker and balance bike at layer 10.
- Critic round 1 found that an adult balance bike also fit the old clue 10 ("built for adult riders"). That clue was replaced. Round 2 also flagged "invented before the bicycle", because the dandy horse is itself often called the first bicycle, so it's now "invented before pedal bikes".
- Aliases cover its other names: draisine, draisienne, Laufmaschine, running machine, hobby horse, pedestrian curricle, swiftwalker. "Hobby horse" also means the stick-horse toy, which is harmless here.
- Velocipede is put at depth 5 (pedals, the 1860s boneshaker meaning). The word originally also covered the draisine, so a history buff could argue for it at layer 10 and lose a pickaxe.
- Clue 7 "has handlebars": the dandy horse had a steering bar. A pedant could object, but most players will accept it.
- Clue 8 "exactly two wheels": the generic "scooter" is treated as two-wheeled (depth 8), though kids' three-wheel scooters exist.
- Earlier calls still apply: suitcase, shopping trolley, office chair and trolley are at depth 1 (they don't carry a person); wheelchair and pram fail clue 5.
