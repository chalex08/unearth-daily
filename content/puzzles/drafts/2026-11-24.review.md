# 2026-11-24 (#17, unicycle): judgement calls

- Clue 2 "carries a person": suitcase, shopping trolley, office chair, baby walker and trolley are put at depth 1. A trolley is a streetcar in the US, which does carry people, and kids do ride in shopping trolleys.
- Clue 3 "smaller than a car": forklift, golf cart, rickshaw and go-kart count as smaller. Horse-drawn carriage and dune buggy don't (depth 2).
- Clue 4 "has no motor": wheelchair and scooter are taken as the manual and kick kinds (no motor). The electric versions are separate answers at depth 3, as is e-bike. Rickshaw is at depth 3 as the motor kind; pedicab covers the pedal kind.
- Clue 5 "its rider powers with their legs": the pram (pushed by someone else), wheelchair (arms) and chariot fail. Skateboard, kick scooter and skates pass.
- Clue 8 "built for one rider" only removes tandem. Clue 9 "no giant front wheel" only removes penny-farthing, which is a rare answer. That's fine late in the ladder, but both clues are weak.
- Clue 10 "has no handlebars": some touring unicycles have a T-bar handle. The critic called that borderline. The ultimate wheel is an alias of unicycle.
- Helicopter is at depth 2 (some have wheels, many have skids).
